How we pick

Hype Check is an independent list of TikTok Shop US products, ranked only by public review data.

Where the numbers come from

Price, star rating, review count, units sold and seller name are read from TikTok Shop's public category pages. They are TikTok Shop's own numbers, not ours. The date they were last checked is shown on every page.

The bar a product has to clear

A listing needs at least 25 buyer reviews and a 4.5-star average before it can appear anywhere on this site. Individual guides raise that bar or add a price cap, and say so at the top.

How the ranking works

Products are ordered by a review-weighted rating. Each listing's average is blended with a neutral 4.5-star baseline worth 50 reviews, so a 5.0 from 30 buyers does not outrank a 4.9 from 2,000. Ties go to the listing with more units sold. The one exception is the most-bought guide, which sorts by units sold.

What this site does not do

  • We do not buy or test the products. This is a filter over public feedback, not a hands-on review.
  • Commission never changes a rank. See the affiliate disclosure.
  • We leave out food, supplements and remedies for people or pets, where reviews can't verify the claims, along with weapons, alcohol-themed and adult items, and chance-based "break" sales.
  • Prices and stock change quickly. The TikTok Shop listing is always the source of truth.
